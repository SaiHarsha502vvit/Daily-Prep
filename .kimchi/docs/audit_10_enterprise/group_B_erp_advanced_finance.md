# Group B Audit: ERP Advanced + Finance Core + Finance Real-World

## Overall Verdict

This group has strong bones but structural damage that causes teaching friction. 03_ERP_Finance_Core.md is the group's intellectual anchor — it has the best conceptual framing, the best topical breadth, and the best historical-evolution narrative of all three files. 02_ERP_Advanced.md is the broadest in scope but suffers from broken file beginnings (starts mid-content, no introduction), inconsistent challenge blocks, and very thin conceptual-depth paragraphs. 04_ERP_Finance_RealWorld.md is the most pedagogically ambitious (six learning-technique sections, a 30-day plan) but is undermined by a SQL window-function contamination challenge and a large orphaned bulk-insert code block at the top. All three files lack the canonical 5W1H framing block at the top, which per the standards is a preservation requirement for this curriculum. The cross-file flow (02→03→04) is coherent overall — it tracks a learner from ERP integration patterns through finance fundamentals to real-world scenarios. Fix the structural damage first, then address the per-file weaknesses.

## Cross-file flow observations

**What works:**
- The reading order 02 (ERP Advanced) → 03 (Finance Core) → 04 (Finance Real-World) is logical. 02 establishes the ERP integration landscape (modules, workflows, orders, inventory), 03 drills into the finance domain (GL, AP, AR, tax), and 04 connects DSA to finance production scenarios. A learner who reads all three in order gets a coherent narrative.
- The Historical Evolution sections are mutually reinforcing: 02 traces SAP integration history (1968 EDI → 2017 Kafka), 03 traces accounting history (1494 Pacioli → 2018 continuous accounting), and 04 traces the regulatory frameworks that constrain all three (SOX 2002 → PSD2 2018). Together they give a learner the full temporal context.
- The SAP Finance Modules table in 03 and the SAP Integration Patterns table in 02 are complementary — FI/CO/AP/AR in 03, and the integration layer (RFC/BAPI/IDoc) in 02.

**What doesn't work:**
- **Missing 5W1H blocks across all three files** — none of the three has the canonical framing block. This is the single most damaging consistency gap in the group. The preservation guarantee in PEDAGOGY_STANDARDS.md explicitly requires the 5W1H block in every chapter.
- **Challenge density is wildly inconsistent**: 02 has one challenge for 9 parts; 03 has one challenge for 6 term categories; 04 has two challenges (one of which is contaminated). A learner who relies on section challenges for active recall gets almost nothing from this group.
- **No cross-file challenge continuity**: A challenge in 02 about inventory reservation could build toward a challenge in 04 about phantom inventory (a failure mode). A challenge in 03 about debit/credit could connect to 04's reconciliation two-pointers. Currently, challenges are isolated within files.
- **The DSA content is split awkwardly**: 03 says "Finance, Real-World DSA" in its title but contains no DSA. The DSA is in 04. The title of 03 is misleading and should be either "ERP Finance Core" (current) or the DSA should be moved to a later file and 03 should be retitled accordingly.
- **Structural damage in 04**: The orphaned bulk-insert block at the top of 04 makes it look like a draft that was never finalized. Combined with the contaminated SQL challenge, this file reads as less polished than the other two.
